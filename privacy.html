The short version: Parcel Loop does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or other contact details to play. Some third-party services help us understand crashes and gameplay, deliver ads, send notifications, support leaderboards and process purchases.

01Information kept on your device

Your progress, stars, coins, gems, lives, power-ups, belt themes, settings and the factory name you choose are saved on your device only. We do not receive this information. Deleting the game deletes it.

02Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played and to make it better. It receives:

  • Gameplay events, such as levels started and finished, results, stars, scores, mistakes, time played, power-ups used, coins and gems earned and spent, purchases made, ads watched, settings changed and screens opened.
  • A random app instance identifier, your device model, operating system version, app version, language, and your approximate country and region, worked out from your IP address.

This information is not used to identify you personally and is never sold. Google keeps analytics data for up to 14 months.

03Crash reports

If the game crashes or hits an error, Firebase Crashlytics sends a report with technical details: the error, the state of the app, your device model, operating system version and a random installation identifier. We use it only to find and fix problems.

04Advertising

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show, limit and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), your IP address and approximate location, and information about the ads you see and tap.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and where other laws require it, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings > Privacy choices.
  • On iPhone and iPad, the advertising identifier is only used for tracking if you allow it when the game asks. You can change this at any time in your device's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's settings.
Rewarded ads are optional. You choose to watch one in exchange for a reward. Buying “No Ads” removes the ads shown between levels.

With your tracking permission, the game also uses what happens in it, such as your coins and recent results, on your device to decide when to offer extras like double coins. This information does not leave your device.

05Push notifications

If you allow notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging gives your device a token so that we can send you news and reminders about the game. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device's settings.

06Leaderboards

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center or Google Play Games, your score (the number of parcels you have shipped) is sent to Apple or Google together with your player profile, and shown to other players on the leaderboard. Your player name and profile are managed by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies.

07Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive your payment details. We receive only a confirmation of what was bought, so the game can deliver it and restore it.

08Why we use information (legal bases)

Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation applies, we rely on:

  • Performance of our agreement with you to run the game, deliver purchases and show leaderboards.
  • Our legitimate interests to understand how the game is played, keep it working and fix crashes.
  • Your consent for personalised advertising, tracking and push notifications. You can withdraw consent at any time as described above.

09Who receives information

Information is processed by the service providers named below, acting for us or as independent controllers:

  • Google LLC (Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Cloud Messaging, AdMob, Google Play Games).
  • Apple Inc. (App Store, Game Center).

These providers may process information in the United States and other countries, under safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for any other purpose.

10Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act or the Brazilian LGPD), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to or restrict its use, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Residents of California may opt out of the “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising by declining tracking or personalised ads as described in section 4.

Because the game has no accounts, we cannot link analytics data to you by name. To make a request, contact us and include the device you play on; we will help as far as we can identify the data. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

11Children

Parcel Loop is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

12Security and retention

Information sent by the game travels encrypted. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days, after which they are deleted by Google. Information on your device stays until you delete the game.

13Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The new version will be published in the game and on our website with a new effective date. Significant changes will be announced in the game.
